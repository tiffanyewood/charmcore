The Details

Everything parents need to know before booking.

What age group is CharmCore Co. best for? ▼

Our craft bars are specially curated for ages 8 through 16 (tweens and teens), however we can accommodate any age from 2-102! Materials and difficulty levels are adapted based on the specific age of the birthday guest.

What space or equipment do I need to provide? ▼

We bring all craft supplies, folding tables and coverings, and tools. We just ask for space to set up and nearby electrical outlets depending on the craft chosen.

What is your minimum guest headcount? ▼

Our standard packages cover up to 10 guests. Additional guests can be added to any package for an additional fee.

What is the cost for a party? ▼

Our party packages start at $350 for up to 10 guests. Additional guests can be added to any package for a fee.

How long does a craft bar experience last? ▼

Our craft bar experiences typically take 45-90 minutes to complete. Pre-party setup time is typically 60-90 minutes.
